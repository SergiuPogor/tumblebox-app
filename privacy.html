Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Version 1.3

This policy explains how the Tumblebox Android app and our support email handle information. Tumblebox is provided by MEDIAPRONET SRL (Societatea cu Răspundere Limitată MEDIAPRONET), a limited liability company registered in the Republic of Moldova with company ID (IDNO) 1012600005500 ("we", "us"), represented by its CEO, Serghei Pogor. Contact: mediapronetsrl@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Tumblebox runs entirely on your phone. It has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your scores, progress or settings anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share personal data through the app.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Android may include it in your own device backup; the random number the app makes for itself is never backed up.
  • If optional purchases are offered, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

What the app keeps on your phone

The app saves the following in its private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it:

  • Scores and best scores, your level and XP, badges, medals, titles and your Play Style
  • Daily Drop results and your streak, Weekly Mix tries, puzzles solved and how many tries each took
  • Your Album cards, costumes, skins, coins and Boosts
  • Daily quests, the daily crate, the Weekly Feat, and your progress in seasonal events (their quests and card goals)
  • Your settings (theme, sound, music, vibration, reduce motion, seasonal looks) and how far the first-run hints have got
  • The shop items you asked to be told about with "Notify me", so the app can show them when the shop opens
  • Promo codes you have entered: which ones were used, today's promo Boosts, and a short pause after several wrong tries
  • Rival names you type in Enter code, and your head-to-head record (wins and losses) for your last 50 challenges. A rival name stays on your phone: it is never put in a challenge code, a shared message or a clip.
  • A random number made on your phone the first time the app runs (the "install salt"). It varies your daily quests and the contents of earned boxes. It never leaves your phone: it is not in challenge codes, links, shared pictures or backups.
  • Video clips you make with Make a clip. They are made on your phone from your own run, kept in the app's cache folder and deleted after a day. They only leave your phone if you share them through Android's share menu.

The app's cache folder also holds the game sounds, which the app generates itself. Android may clear that folder at any time.

This information is under your control. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings (Settings › Apps › Tumblebox › Storage › Clear storage), removes all of it. As it never leaves your device and we have no access to it, we do not collect it or otherwise process it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use the internet. The app has no INTERNET permission.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in or cloud save.
  • It shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It reads the phone's motion sensor only while Home is open, and only to make the characters tumble when you shake the phone. Nothing from it is stored or sent.
  • It does not profile you or make automated decisions about you. Your Play Style and medals are worked out on your phone, for the game only, and have no effect outside it.

Clipboard

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste in Enter code, to fill in a challenge code or promo code you copied. It uses that text only to fill in the field, on your phone, and does not store it or send it anywhere. If you use Help and feedback and your phone has no email app, the app copies our email address to the clipboard so that you can paste it into any email. The app copies nothing else to the clipboard.

Permissions

The app uses these Android permissions. None of them gives the app internet access:

  • Vibration (VIBRATE): short buzzes when pieces land and rows clear. You can turn vibration off, or make it gentler, in Settings.
  • Google Play purchases (com.android.vending.BILLING): lets Google Play's purchase screen talk to the app on the phone, for the optional purchases. Google Play handles the payment; the app itself has no internet access.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): added by a Google library that comes with Google Play purchases. It lets that library see whether the phone is online. It gives no internet access, and the app sends nothing.

Android may also list app.tumblebox.game.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: the app grants it only to itself, for messages inside the app. If we add an optional reminder, the app will also use the notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, which Android asks you for first). It gives no internet access either. We will update this policy before such a version is released.

Device backups

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup, so that it comes back on a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google (or your device maker) under your own account and its privacy policy. We cannot access it. The install salt is excluded from backups: a restored phone gets a new one. You can turn backup off, or delete a backup, in your phone's settings.

Sharing your results

Sharing is optional. When you tap Share, the app makes a picture, a video clip or text on your phone, and Android's share menu sends it to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you choose to. A shared link only carries tags that tell us which kind of moment was shared, never anything about you. Once you post something to another service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Google Play

We distribute Tumblebox through Google Play. Google processes information when you download, update or rate apps, as described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics, Google may show us totals and crash reports without names in Play Console. We do not use them to identify anyone.

Purchases

Tumblebox may offer optional purchases, such as Boost packs, cosmetic items and the Tumblebox Plus subscription. The Terms of Use explain them.

  • Google Play handles payment: Google Play collects and processes your payment details as a separate controller, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice (https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice). We never receive your card or bank details.
  • On your phone: Google Play tells the app on your phone what you bought. The app checks this on the phone and saves it there. It does not send it to us or to anyone else.
  • In Play Console: Google gives us limited records of each order, such as the order number, product, price, date, country or region, state or province and postal code (where Google provides them) and status. We use them only to handle refunds and support requests, to prevent fraud and to keep our accounts. The legal bases are performing our contract with you and meeting our accounting and tax duties (Article 6(1)(b) and (c) of the GDPR), and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and in establishing, exercising or defending legal claims (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). We keep these records for as long as accounting and tax law requires, and then delete them.

When you email us

If you write to mediapronetsrl@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, your message and any attachments. We use them only to answer you and to deal with what you asked.

  • Legal basis: taking the steps you ask for, and our legitimate interest in answering messages and keeping a record of them (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) of the GDPR, and the matching rules of Moldovan law).

Under your message the draft lists the app version, the Android version, the phone model and the app language, so that we can look into a problem. The app sends nothing itself: you see the whole draft, can change or delete any of it, and nothing reaches us unless you press send.

Google provides this service under its own terms and privacy policy, as an independent provider, and may store messages on servers outside the Republic of Moldova and the EU. Where messages are transferred to Google LLC in the United States, Google states that it relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and on Standard Contractual Clauses, as Google describes at https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks
  • How long: we delete support emails within 24 months after the conversation ends, unless we need them longer to deal with a legal claim or because the law requires it.
