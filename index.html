Tumblebox

A falling-block puzzle where every level pushes the walls out, and the box grows wider.

Coming soon to Google Play

A box that grows

Every level pushes the walls out by one column on each side, from 8 up to 16.

Rows and spans

Fill a row, or link one kind of character from wall to wall. Cleared cells fall and can cascade.

Four worlds

Fruit ripens, veggies sprout, robots spark and aliens beam up your tallest column.

Seven ways to play

Endless, Daily Drop, Rush 180, Zen Box, Weekly Mix, Boss Board and 30 Puzzles.

Play with friends

Share your Daily Drop card, or send a challenge code with the same pieces.

Private and offline

No internet permission, no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Progress stays on your phone.
