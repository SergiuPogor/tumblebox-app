Playing safely

Tumblebox uses bright colours, moving characters, confetti, flashes and vibration. Large flashes are limited to at most three a second. If you have epilepsy or are sensitive to flashing lights, talk to a doctor before you play, and stop at once if you feel unwell. Reduce motion in Settings turns off screen shake and slow motion and cuts particles. Take regular breaks.
