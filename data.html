Your Data and Deletion

Last updated: 2026-11-10 · Version 1.0

This page explains where your Tumblebox data is and how to delete it or ask for a copy. Tumblebox is provided by MEDIAPRONET SRL (Societatea cu Răspundere Limitată MEDIAPRONET), a limited liability company registered in the Republic of Moldova with company ID (IDNO) 1012600005500 ("we", "us"), represented by its CEO, Serghei Pogor. Contact: mediapronetsrl@gmail.com. The Privacy Policy has the full details.

The short version

  • Tumblebox has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.
  • Your game data is only on your phone. You can delete it yourself at any time.
  • We hold personal data only if you emailed us or bought something. You can ask us to delete it, correct it or send you a copy.

Delete the game data on your phone

Your progress, items and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see or delete them for you. To delete them:

  1. Open Android Settings › Apps › Tumblebox › Storage and tap Clear storage. This deletes everything and keeps the app installed.
  2. Or uninstall the app. This deletes everything too.

This cannot be undone. Items you bought once (such as Plus Lifetime, skins or costumes) can be restored from Google Play. Coins, Boosts and progress cannot.

Delete your device backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of your progress in your own Google account, so that it comes back on a new phone. We cannot access that copy. You can delete it, or turn backup off, in your phone's settings under Google › Backup, or in Google Drive under Backups.

Delete what we hold

  • Support emails: if you wrote to us, we have your email address and your messages. Ask us and we will delete them. We delete them in any case within 24 months after the conversation ends.
  • Order records: if you bought something, Google Play gives us a record of the order, without your payment details. Accounting and tax law requires us to keep these records for a set time, so we cannot delete them early. We delete them when that time ends.
  • Your Google account: Google holds its own records of your downloads and purchases. You manage them in your Google account, at https://myaccount.google.com

How to send a request

Email mediapronetsrl@gmail.com from the address you used to contact us, and tell us what you want: a copy of your data, a correction or deletion. For an order, add the order number from your Google Play receipt. We reply within one month, and may ask you to confirm your identity first. A request is free.

Your other rights

You can also object to how we use your data, ask us to restrict its use, and complain to a data protection authority. In the Republic of Moldova this is the National Center for Personal Data Protection (NCPDP), https://datepersonale.md. The Privacy Policy lists all your rights and the other authorities.
