Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10 · Version 1.0

This policy explains whether the Tumblebox Android app and our website use cookies or similar technologies. Tumblebox is provided by MEDIAPRONET SRL (Societatea cu Răspundere Limitată MEDIAPRONET), a limited liability company registered in the Republic of Moldova with company ID (IDNO) 1012600005500 ("we", "us"), represented by its CEO, Serghei Pogor. The short version

  • The Tumblebox app uses no cookies.
  • Our website sets no cookies.
  • We use no advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, analytics or other tracking tools.
  • That is why you see no cookie banner: there is nothing to ask your consent for.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website or app saves on your device, usually to remember you between visits or to follow what you do. Similar technologies do the same job in other ways, for example advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, and software from other companies built into an app.

The app

The app has no permission to use the internet, shows no web pages inside itself and has no accounts. It cannot set or read cookies, and it contains no advertising or analytics software. It does not use the Android advertising ID.

What the app saves on your phone

The app saves your game on your phone: your progress, your items and your settings. This is not a cookie and it is not used to track you. It is needed for the game you asked for to work, it stays in the app's private storage, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. The Privacy Policy lists everything that is saved. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of it.

Our website

Our website, https://sergiupogor.github.io/tumblebox-app/, sets no cookies, runs no scripts, has no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep its service secure, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Services of other companies

Some things you can do from the app open another app or service, which has its own rules on cookies:

  • Google Play: the store page, purchases, subscriptions and ratings are handled by Google Play, under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's page about cookies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).
  • Links: a web link in the app opens in your own browser, which follows its own settings.
  • Sharing and email: when you share a result or write to us, the app you choose handles it under its own terms.

We do not control these services and receive no cookie data from them.

Your choices

There is nothing to switch off in Tumblebox, because nothing is switched on. You can manage cookies for other apps and websites in your browser and in your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If we ever wanted to use cookies or similar technologies, we would update this page and the date at the top first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before using them.
