Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how to cancel a Tumblebox Plus subscription and how to get a refund for a purchase made in the Tumblebox app. Tumblebox is provided by MEDIAPRONET SRL (Societatea cu Răspundere Limitată MEDIAPRONET), a limited liability company registered in the Republic of Moldova with company ID (IDNO) 1012600005500 ("we", "us"), represented by its CEO, Serghei Pogor. Contact: mediapronetsrl@gmail.com. It is part of the Terms of Use. If the two differ, the Terms of Use apply.

The short version

  • Tumblebox is free to play. Every purchase is optional.
  • Google Play takes the payment, so refunds are paid back through Google Play.
  • You can cancel Tumblebox Plus at any time in Google Play. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
  • If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal.
  • If something went wrong with a purchase, email us and we will help.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Ask Google Play first. Open your order history in Google Play, or use Google's refund page: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637
  2. If Google Play cannot help, or sends you to the developer, email mediapronetsrl@gmail.com. Tell us the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA) and what went wrong.
  3. We answer as quickly as we can. When a refund is approved, Google Play pays it back to the payment method you used.

We never see your card or bank details, so we cannot pay a refund in any other way.

Your right of withdrawal

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may withdraw from a purchase of digital content or a subscription within 14 days, without giving a reason. Google Play's checkout handles this. For digital content you may be asked to agree that delivery starts at once. After that, the right of withdrawal ends for that content, as the law allows. To use your right, ask for a refund in Google Play or email us within the 14 days.

Cancelling Tumblebox Plus

  • How to cancel: open Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose Tumblebox Plus and tap Cancel. In the app, Settings also has a Manage subscription link that opens the same place.
  • What happens next: Plus stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Then it stops renewing and you are not charged again.
  • Uninstalling is not cancelling: removing the app from your phone does not stop the subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.
  • Free trial: where a free trial is offered, cancel before it ends and you are not charged.
  • Part of a period: cancelling does not refund the days left in a period you have paid for, unless the law gives you that right or one of the cases below applies.

One-time purchases

Plus Lifetime, skins, costumes, packs and coins are paid once and do not renew. Ask for a refund as described above. When a purchase is refunded, what it unlocked is taken back from the game. Things you earned by playing are never taken away.

If something does not work

If something you bought was not delivered, or does not work as described, email us. We will deliver it again, fix it or refund it. Consumers in the European Union, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Moldova also have the rights the law gives for digital content that does not conform to the contract. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

If we change or stop Tumblebox

  • Plus subscription: if we remove a paid feature, or stop offering the app, while you have a paid Plus period running, we will refund the unused part of that period.
  • One-time purchases: if we stop offering the app within 24 months after a one-time purchase, we will refund the part of the price that matches the rest of those 24 months, or more where the law requires it.
  • Consumers in the EU, EEA and UK: if a change has more than a minor negative effect on digital content you paid for, you may end the contract free of charge within 30 days of the change or of our notice of it, whichever is later, and get a refund for the part you can no longer use.

What cannot be refunded

Coins, Boosts, cards and other items you earned by playing or received from a free promo code were not bought and have no cash value, so there is nothing to refund for them.
